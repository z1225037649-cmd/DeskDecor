## Defect reproduced and fixed in source

The saved first group was 208 × 1213 at DPI 120, taller than the desktop. Without user input, its settings file was rewritten at approximately two-second intervals (observed timestamps 17:48:18.458, 17:48:20.456 and 17:48:22.453, Asia/Shanghai).

The refresh timer first clamped the group to the monitor working area. `LayoutItemsInGroup` then expanded its single-column content back beyond the screen height, causing recovery and settings saves on every subsequent timer tick.

The fix considers content size during recovery and widens tall narrow groups when their entries fit in more columns. All groups are laid out after startup recovery, including inactive groups. If there are too many entries for the entire monitor, the required content height is retained to avoid repeated shrink/grow cycles; scrolling or pagination remains outside this fix.

`OverlayLayoutRegression.cs` uses the real hidden `OverlayForm` layout method without creating a desktop window. The new test failed before the fix and passed afterward. It covers 12 entries at 125% and 150%, smaller and offset working areas, inactive groups, and a content-capacity overflow case.
